## About Supertec

Supertec Machines combines engineering expertise, modern manufacturing technology, and customer-focused solutions to meet the evolving needs of the global wire and cable industry.

Based in Faridabad, Haryana, India, we provide machinery solutions for a wide range of cable manufacturing applications, from individual production units to complete extrusion lines.

Our focus is on precision, productivity, durability, and dependable technical support.

## Our Products

- **Cable & Wire Extrusion Lines** — Advanced extrusion systems for diverse cable manufacturing requirements.
- **SIOPLAS Insulation Lines** — Specialized extrusion solutions for XLPE power cable production.
- **Triple-Layer Extrusion Lines** — Multi-layer insulation systems for high-tension cable applications.
- **Building Wire Insulation Lines** — High-performance solutions for building and automotive wires.
- **Take-Up & Pay-Off Systems** — Reliable cable handling and tension-control equipment.
- **Extruders & Crossheads** — Precision-engineered components for consistent insulation and sheathing.
- **Rewinding & Cable Machinery** — Equipment designed for efficient cable processing and production.
